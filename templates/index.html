See what's growing, from space.

Point at any farm on Earth and a date. Canopy pulls a Sentinel-2 NDVI time series through Google Earth Engine, extracts the crop's growth lifecycle, classifies the crop, and reads out its vegetation health — Tomato, Cotton, Paddy, Sugarcane, or Onion.

Tip: choosing Sugarcane as the crop hint extracts a full ±12 month window from Earth Engine (instead of ±6) so its ~13–14 month lifecycle is captured completely.

⤒

Drop a CSV or Excel file or click to browse

Columns needed: Latitude, Longitude, and a GroundTruth_Date (or Date) column. Farm_ID and Crop are optional. Up to 200 rows per file.

Querying Earth Engine and classifying each point — this can take a little while for many rows.

NDVI health scale

Canopy runs Sentinel-2 imagery through Google Earth Engine and a trained crop-lifecycle classifier. Predictions depend on cloud-free imagery availability for the chosen location/date.
